\item \textbf{Inference efficiency:} first author of SliderField, a training-free sampler that predicts skipped flow-model steps with a Gaussian random field; 4--5$\times$ fewer model calls on image and video models (FLUX, Qwen-Image-Edit, Wan2.1), 3.5--27$\times$ closer to full computation than the best caching baseline.
