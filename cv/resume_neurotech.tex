\item \textbf{Predicting stimulation responses:} multi-animal models trained on optogenetic (\textit{C.~elegans}, 113 animals) and holographic photostimulation (mouse motor cortex) data predict responses to never-stimulated targets; toward in-silico testing of stimulation and closed-loop control.
